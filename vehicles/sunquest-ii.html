Sunquest II (1993)

Built in 1993 from SunQuest I‘s molds, SunQuest II, affectionately known as Big Yellow, placed 14th out of 36 entries at Sunrayce ’93. This North American collegiate race started in Dallas, Texas and ended in Minneapolis, Minnesota.
